How to play
  • Two boxes float in front of you: pink for the left hand, blue for the right. A hand only plays while its palm is inside its box.
  • Move inside the box for XY, push forward for depth, tilt the knuckles for roll, pinch thumb and index for the pinch CC.
  • Flick right index, middle or ring finger toward your palm for drum taps.
  • Left fist = shift: the right box switches to its second pair of CCs.
  • Hand shapes (thumb up/down, open palm, fist, victory, pointing up, I love you) fire gate notes.
  • The button strip on your left: Panic, Mode, Recenter (moves the boxes to where you look), Mute, HUD. Poke with a fingertip.
  • On a keyboard: P panic, M mode, R recenter, Space mute, H HUD.
